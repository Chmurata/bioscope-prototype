Package Selection & Subscribe

A mobile package-selection screen for an OTT aggregator — OTT bundles, combo & data packs, with mobile balance, wallet, card and voucher payments.

Key features

Filter by validity & platform

Tabs for 1 Day to 365 Days, and chips for StreamMax, PlayArena, DesiFlix, Combo and Data+OTT.

One recommended pack

A highlighted "Most Popular" combo pack sits above the filtered list.

Mixed pack types

Pure OTT bundles, OTT+TVOD combos, and data+OTT bundles side by side.

Payment method per pack

Mobile balance, wallet/MFS, card and voucher shown per pack — only the methods that pack supports.
